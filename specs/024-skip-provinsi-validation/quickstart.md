# Quickstart & Validation Guide: Skip SK CPCL & Verification Validation in Kabupaten & Provinsi Detail Views

## Scenario 1: Verify Dinas Kabupaten Step 1 Skip Validation
1. Log in as Dinas Kabupaten and view a proposal queue detail.
2. Verify that the "Simpan & Lanjut ke SK CPCL" button is enabled (colored green and clickable) immediately.
3. Click the button and confirm it navigates directly to Step 3 ("SK CPCL") without requiring any checklist approvals or opening confirmation modals.

## Scenario 2: Verify Dinas Kabupaten Step 3 Skip Validation
1. Go to step 3 ("SK CPCL").
2. Verify that the next button is enabled and clickable immediately.
3. Click it and confirm it navigates directly to Step 4 ("Summary & Submit") without requiring document uploads.

## Scenario 3: Verify Dinas Provinsi Step 3 Skip Validation
1. Log in as Dinas Provinsi and view a proposal queue detail.
2. Go to step 3 ("Surat Pengantar SK CPCL").
3. Verify that the "Simpan & Lanjut ke Summary & Submit" button is enabled and clickable immediately.
4. Click it and confirm it goes directly to Step 4 ("Summary & Submit") without requiring document uploads or verification checkmarks.
